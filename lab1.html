Research Experience

My research spans structural biochemistry, computational genomics, and biomedical technology.

Structural Biochemistry and Protein Engineering

Studied the structure and catalytic mechanism of OhmK, a heme enzyme involved in tryptophan hydroxylation, using experimental and computational approaches.

Methods & Tools
  • Protein purification and characterization
  • Structural modeling
  • Molecular docking
  • Molecular dynamics simulations

Computational Genomics

Investigated the genetic determinants of serum albumin and its causal relationship with cardiovascular disease using large-scale population datasets.

Methods & Tools
  • Large-scale biobank data
  • Genome-wide association studies (GWAS)
  • Mendelian randomization (MR)
  • Statistical genomics

Mixed Reality fNIRS Optode Positioning

Developed a mixed reality approach for fNIRS optode positioning by integrating neuroimaging data, 3D modeling, and photon transport simulations to guide optode placement.

Methods & Tools
  • MRI-based 3D head modeling
  • fMRI-defined brain regions
  • Photon transport simulations
  • Mixed reality visualization
