About Me

My name is Jingo Tu. I am a Bioengineering master's student at UC Berkeley. I focus on Computational Biology and Bioinformatics.

Research Experience

Structural Biochemistry and Protein Engineering

Studied the structure and catalytic mechanism of OhmK, a heme enzyme involved in tryptophan hydroxylation, using protein characterization, structural modeling, molecular docking, and molecular dynamics simulations.

Computational Genomics

Investigated the genetic determinants of serum albumin and its causal relationship with cardiovascular disease using large-scale biobank data, genome-wide association studies (GWAS), and Mendelian randomization (MR).

Mixed Reality fNIRS Optode Positioning

Developed a mixed reality approach for fNIRS optode positioning by integrating MRI-based 3D head models, fMRI-defined brain regions, and photon transport simulations to guide optode placement.
